# 유베이스 - \[급여명세서/대장출력\] - 급여명세서

sp : ubase_SXPRPayReceiptPrint2021

 

1.  \_SXPRPayResultPrtEmpItem 서브 sp를 통해 출력항목생성

    1.  근태항목의 경우 \[월근태집계내역조회\] 에서 확인이 가능

        1.  SELECT PP.EmpSeq , B.ItemSeq,

> SUM(PP.DTCnt), MAX(B.PosNum) , 0
>
> FROM \_TPRPayEmpMm AS PP WITH(NOLOCK)
>
> INNER JOIN \_TXPRBasPrtTypeItem AS B WITH(NOLOCK)
>
> ON PP.CompanySeq = B.CompanySeq
>
> AND PP.WkItemSeq = B.ItemSeq
>
> INNER JOIN \_TXPRBasPrtType AS A WITH(NOLOCK) -- \_TXPRBasPrtTypePb -\> \_TXPRBasPrtType 테이블 변경
>
> ON B.CompanySeq = A.CompanySeq
>
> AND B.PrtTypeSeq = A.PrtTypeSeq
>
> INNER JOIN \_TPRWkItem AS PW WITH(NOLOCK)
>
> ON PW.WkItemSeq = B.ItemSeq
>
> AND PW.CompanySeq = B.CompanySeq
>
> AND (PW.SMWkItemType IN (3007001 , 3007002) OR PW.WkItemSeq = -1) -- 시스템월일수 추가
>
> WHERE PP.CompanySeq = 1
>
> AND PP.PbYm = '202401'
>
> AND PP.SerialNo = 16
>
> AND B.ItemType = 'W'
>
> AND A.PrtTypeSeq = 5
>
> AND A.SMPrtType = 3041002
>
> AND PP.EmpSeq = 47249
>
> GROUP BY PP.EmpSeq, B.ItemSeq
>
>  

2.  항목 목록은 \[출력유형등록\]에 있다

    1.  \_TXPRBasPrtTypeItem ( SELECT \* FROM \_TPRPayEmpMm WHERE EmpSeq = 47249 AND PbYm = '202401' )

        1.  ItemSeq = 근태항목코드

            1.  근무일수, 근무시간 =\> SELECT \* FROM \_TXPRBasPrtTypeItem WHERE ItemSeq IN(1000,999) AND PosNum IN(101,102) AND PrtTypeSeq = 5
